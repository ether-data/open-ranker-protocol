## Why dual-license

The specification text is under CC BY 4.0 because that is the license most appropriate for standards documents — it permits the widest possible adoption while requiring attribution. The reference implementations, schemas, and code (under `LICENSE`, Apache 2.0) are under a different license because that license is most appropriate for software — it grants explicit patent rights and permits commercial use without onerous redistribution requirements.
